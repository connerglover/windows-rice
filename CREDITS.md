## Foundations and design inspiration

- [MrDLingters / EverforestWin11](https://github.com/MrDLingters/EverforestWin11) — original colors and wallpaper source. Imported revision: `88f430bd2d1d32a9ba3070f4531ece36c96e133c`.
- [MrDLingters / Win11EverforestDarkSoft](https://github.com/MrDLingters/Win11EverforestDarkSoft) — original rice configuration. Imported revision: `61741f522dddc5116584fc35f6703ee5c2401492`.
- [sainnhe / Everforest](https://github.com/sainnhe/everforest) — the Everforest palette and theme family.
- [Ryoku navigation](https://docs.ryoku.dev/docs/navigation) — navigation model; adapted here to Alt, with Alt+W to close and direct resize shortcuts.
- [az3l9882 / azelismybagle-windows11-rice](https://github.com/az3l9882/azelismybagle-windows11-rice) — Flow Launcher layout inspiration.
- [refact0r / System24](https://github.com/refact0r/system24) — Vencord theme foundation; original attribution remains in the CSS.
- [ful1e5 / Bibata Cursor](https://github.com/ful1e5/Bibata_Cursor) — cursor design, adapted to sage. Also see [KevinSilvester’s Windows distribution](https://github.com/KevinSilvester/bibata-cursor).
- Wallpaper photographers, artists and uploaders on [Wallhaven](https://wallhaven.cc) — artwork is not original to this project. See the asset list.
